Backtr

Test any stock strategy. See real results.

A free iOS app for backtesting trading strategies against historical market data.

Backtr lets you pick a ticker, choose a strategy — MACD, RSI, Bollinger Bands, moving average crossover, breakout, or an ensemble vote — and see how it would have performed historically, including transaction costs, drawdown, Sharpe/Sortino ratios, and a comparison against buy-and-hold and the S&P 500.

Not financial advice Backtr is built for education and entertainment only. Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. See the Terms of Service for details.
