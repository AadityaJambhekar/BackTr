Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Backtr iOS application (the "App") collects, how it is used, and your choices. Backtr is an independent, individually-developed application.

Summary

Backtr does not collect, store, or sell any personal or identifiable information about you. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers of any kind built into the App.

Information the App Sends

To run a backtest, the App sends the stock ticker, date range, strategy selection, and starting capital you enter to Backtr's own backend server, which uses this information solely to calculate and return the results to you. This data is not linked to your identity — the App does not ask for or transmit your name, email address, location, contacts, or any device identifier.

When you use the ticker search feature, your search text is sent to Backtr's backend to look up matching stock symbols, for the same purpose.

Market Data

Backtr's backend retrieves historical and current market price data from Yahoo Finance to perform calculations. This is public market data and is not personal information.

Data Stored On Your Device

Backtests you choose to save are stored locally on your device only, in the App's private storage. This data is never uploaded, synced, or shared — it stays on your device and is removed if you delete the App.

Server Logs

Like virtually all internet services, Backtr's backend hosting provider may retain standard server logs (such as IP address and request timestamps) for a limited time for security and operational purposes. These logs are not used to build a profile of you and are not shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Backtr is not directed at children under 13, and the App does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated Effective Date above.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the developer via GitHub.
