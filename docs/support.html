Report a Bug or Ask a Question

The fastest way to reach the developer is to open an issue on GitHub. Please include:

  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead
  • Your iOS version and device model, if relevant

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Backtr giving me investment advice?
No. Backtr simulates how a strategy would have performed on historical data, for education and entertainment. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. See the Terms of Service for details.

Why does a ticker fail to load?
Backtr pulls data from Yahoo Finance. If a ticker is misspelled, delisted, or has too little trading history for the selected date range, the backtest can't run. Try a well-known ticker (e.g. AAPL, MSFT, SPY) to confirm the app and your connection are working.

Where are my saved backtests stored?
Locally on your device only. They are not uploaded anywhere, and are removed if you delete the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
